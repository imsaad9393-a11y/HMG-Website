What this website collects

This website has no forms, accounts or comment boxes, so it does not ask you to type personal information into it. It does not use analytics or advertising tools.

Like most websites, the company that hosts it may keep basic technical records, such as your IP address and the pages you open, to keep the site running and secure.

Google Maps

The Contact page shows a map from Google. When the map loads, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and may use cookies, under Google’s own privacy policy.

When you call, message or email us

The Call, WhatsApp and email links open your phone, WhatsApp or email app. When you contact us, we receive what you choose to tell us, such as your name, phone number and message. We use it to answer your enquiry. WhatsApp and email providers handle messages under their own privacy policies.

Health information

Pages on this site give general information only. They are not medical advice. If you include health details in a message, they pass through the service you use to send it.
