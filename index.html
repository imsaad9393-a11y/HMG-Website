About HMG

HMG Diagnostic & Research Center is a molecular and genomic centre in Peshawar. It works on genetic testing, sequencing and the interpretation of results, with the aim of improving health through genetics.

When you contact us

  1. Call or message us with your question.
  2. We explain the service you asked about.
  3. We tell you what to do next.

Common questions

Do I need a referral?

No. You do not need a referral.

How long do results take?

It depends on the test. Some tests take about a day and others take about a week. Ask us about the test you need.

How do I prepare for a test?

There is no fixed preparation. Please follow your doctor’s guidance for the test you need. If you have a question, call or message us.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day of the week, including Sunday, from 10 am to 8 pm.
