Genetic counselling

What it is

A conversation about genetic conditions, your family history and the testing options that may be available to you. It is not a laboratory test.

Who may ask about it

People with a family history of a genetic condition, couples planning a pregnancy, people who have had a pregnancy screening result, and families with questions about a child's health.

What it cannot tell you

It cannot promise an outcome or make a decision for you. It helps you understand the choices so you can decide together with your doctor.
